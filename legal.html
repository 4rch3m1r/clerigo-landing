Cookie Policy

Technical cookies only: session, authentication and security

Clèrigo XGRC uses technical cookies only: the ones essential to sign in, stay signed in and keep the session safe. Last updated: 20 August 2026.

01 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Alongside cookies we also use similar technologies — local storage (localStorage) and session storage (sessionStorage) — to remember interface preferences.

Cookies can be:

  • Session: deleted automatically when the browser is closed.
  • Persistent: remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them yourself.
  • First-party: set directly by the platform from its own domain. Every cookie we use is of this kind.
  • Third-party: set by external providers. The platform sets none.

The practical difference between a cookie and local storage is where the data goes: a cookie travels to the server on every request; local storage stays in your browser and is never sent. That is why interface preferences are not cookies.

02 Cookies We Use

Every cookie on the platform is strictly necessary: it serves to authenticate you, keep your session and protect it. Without them it is not possible to sign in or use the Service, which is why they are not subject to consent and cannot be switched off from within the platform.

03 What We Do NOT Do

So that it is on the record and does not have to be inferred from an absence:

  • No advertising: we show no ads and use no advertising cookies of any kind.
  • No third-party trackers: we embed no pixels or tags from social networks, advertising platforms or analytics providers.
  • No behavioural analytics: we use no cookies to measure your browsing or to build individual usage profiles.
  • No selling or sharing: we do not sell, rent or share with third parties the data associated with your browsing.
  • No automated decisions based on cookies: no cookie feeds profiles, scores or decisions about you.

That is why the platform shows no cookie consent banner: there are no optional categories to accept or reject. If we were to add any non-essential cookie in future, we would update this policy and ask for your prior consent before enabling it.

04 Managing Your Preferences

Only non-essential cookies can be switched off, and this platform has none. Even so, you keep full control over what is stored in your browser:

  • Your browser settings: you can block or delete cookies and local storage from any browser's privacy settings, at any time.
  • Remembered accounts: removed from the sign-in screen itself, using "Manage", without touching the browser settings.
  • Signing out: signing out invalidates the authentication cookies.
Blocking the essential cookies prevents signing in: without them the server cannot tell that you have already authenticated. Clearing local storage does not stop you using the platform; it only makes it forget your view preferences and remembered accounts.

05 Do Not Track (DNT) Signals and CCPA Rights

Some browsers send "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals to indicate that the user does not wish to be tracked. The platform does not track your behaviour at all, so there is nothing for these signals to switch off: the result is the same whether you send them or not.

For the same reason, the CCPA right to "Do not sell or share my personal information" is met from the outset: we do not sell or share personal information with third parties. Any query on this point can be addressed to the Data Protection Officer named in the Privacy Policy.
